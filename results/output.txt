Difference between stemming and lemmatization:
- Stemming is a rule-based reduction that often cuts words to a rough root, such as 'better' -> 'better' or 'movies' -> 'movi'.
- Lemmatization uses vocabulary and grammar to reduce words to a dictionary form, such as 'better' -> 'good' and 'movies' -> 'movie'.
Lemmatization is preferred because it converts words into meaningful dictionary forms instead of simply cutting off word endings. This helps preserve the semantic meaning of reviews and provides more consistent features for sentiment classification.
